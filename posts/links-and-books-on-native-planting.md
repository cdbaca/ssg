One thing we'd like to do this fall is add some more beds to the backyard. While there are more vegetable / berry areas that we want to add, plus a mini orchard, another thing I would like to do is add some areas with native plants and flowers to do some rewilding and add some areas for local pollinators.

Below are some links and books that I came across when doing some research on the subject:
